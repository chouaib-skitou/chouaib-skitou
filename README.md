## About me

I am a **Full Stack Engineer** with experience building and operating
end-to-end digital products across frontend, backend and platform engineering.

My approach combines product ownership with engineering discipline:
understanding user needs, designing maintainable systems, improving developer
workflows, automating delivery and strengthening application reliability.

I thrive at the intersection of product and engineering, taking ownership from
early requirements and technical design through implementation, deployment and
continuous improvement.

- 🔭 Building full stack products and scalable platform capabilities
- 🧩 Turning complex requirements into maintainable user-facing solutions
- ⚙️ Improving architecture, CI/CD workflows and developer experience
- 📈 Interested in observability, scalability and production reliability
- 💬 Ask me about Angular, TypeScript, Go, Symfony, PostgreSQL or DevOps
